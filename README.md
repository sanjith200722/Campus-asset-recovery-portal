<center><h1><u>ABOUT THE WEBSITE</u></h1></center>
    <p>The Campus Asset Recovery Portal is a prototype web-based application designed to help students and 
        staff report, track, and recover lost or found items within a campus environment. This system bridges
         the gap between individuals who lose items and those who find them by providing a centralized digital
          platform for posting and viewing lost-and-found reports. The portal organizes item details such as 
          item name, category, location, date, description, and contact information, making it easier for users to
           identify and claim their belongings.
    </p>
    <p>
        The system is developed with a focus on simplicity, usability, and accessibility. Users can register, log in securely, 
        and post details about lost or found items, including uploading images for better identification. The platform allows 
        users to filter items based on category and type (lost or found), helping them quickly locate relevant posts. 
        By digitizing the traditional lost-and-found process, the Campus Asset Recovery Portal improves efficiency and 
        reduces the chances of permanently losing valuable items.
    </p>
    <p>
        Additionally, the system includes user profile management and secure authentication features to ensure safe and 
        controlled access. Users can manage their posted items, delete posts if needed, and maintain their account details. 
        The platform promotes responsible community participation by encouraging students and staff to report found items 
        and help return them to rightful owners.
    </p>
    <p>
        The portal also improves campus resource management by reducing manual record-keeping and providing a reliable, searchable 
        database of lost and found items. By offering a centralized and easy-to-use solution, the system enhances trust, communication,
         and cooperation among campus members, creating a safer and more organized campus environment.
    </p>
